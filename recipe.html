Classic Bakery

Rustic Homemade Bread

A golden crust, a soft centre, and the comforting aroma of freshly baked bread.

⏱ Prep: 20 min 🔥 Bake: 30 min 🍞 Serves: 6

Ingredients

  • 500 g plain or bread flour
  • 7 g instant yeast
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 300 ml warm water
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil

Preparation

  1. Mix flour, yeast, sugar, and salt in a bowl.
  2. Add warm water and oil, then knead for 8–10 minutes.
  3. Cover and leave in a warm place until doubled in size, about 1 hour.
  4. Shape the dough and place it in a greased loaf tin.
  5. Allow it to rise for another 25–30 minutes.
  6. Bake at 190°C for 25–30 minutes until golden and cooked through.
  7. Cool before slicing and serving.
French Favourite

Buttery Croissants

Delicate, flaky pastries with buttery layers and a beautifully golden finish.

⏱ Prep: 45 min 🔥 Bake: 20 min 🥐 Makes: 8

Ingredients

  • 500 g plain flour
  • 60 g sugar
  • 7 g instant yeast
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 280 ml cold milk
  • 250 g cold unsalted butter
  • 1 beaten egg for glazing

Preparation

  1. Mix flour, sugar, yeast, salt, and milk into a dough.
  2. Knead until smooth, cover, and refrigerate for at least 1 hour.
  3. Enclose the butter in the dough and roll into a rectangle.
  4. Fold into thirds, chill for 30 minutes, and repeat the rolling and folding twice more.
  5. Roll out, cut into triangles, and shape into croissants.
  6. Proof until puffy, brush with egg, and bake at 200°C for 18–22 minutes.
  7. Cool slightly and enjoy fresh.

Soft Sugar Doughnuts

Light, fluffy doughnuts finished with a sweet coating for a perfect afternoon treat.

⏱ Prep: 25 min 🔥 Cook: 15 min 🍩 Makes: 10

Ingredients

  • 400 g plain flour
  • 7 g instant yeast
  • 60 g sugar
  • 200 ml warm milk
  • 1 egg
  • 50 g softened butter
  • Oil for frying
  • Extra sugar for coating

Preparation

  1. Combine flour, yeast, and sugar.
  2. Add milk and egg, then knead in the butter until smooth.
  3. Cover and let the dough rise for 1 hour.
  4. Roll out and cut into doughnut shapes.
  5. Leave to rise again for 30–45 minutes.
  6. Fry in oil at 170–175°C until golden on both sides.
  7. Drain, cool slightly, and coat with sugar.
Customer Favourite

Chocolate Chip Cookies

Crisp golden edges, soft centres, and rich chocolate in every delicious bite.

⏱ Prep: 15 min 🔥 Bake: 12 min 🍪 Makes: 12

Ingredients

  • 125 g butter, softened
  • 100 g brown sugar
  • 50 g white sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 180 g plain flour
  • ½ teaspoon baking soda
  • 150 g chocolate chips

Preparation

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C and line a baking tray.
  2. Beat butter and both sugars until creamy.
  3. Add the egg and vanilla, mixing well.
  4. Fold in the flour and baking soda, then add chocolate chips.
  5. Place spoonfuls of dough onto the tray, leaving space between each cookie.
  6. Bake for 10–12 minutes until the edges turn golden.
  7. Cool for 5 minutes before transferring to a rack.

Privacy Policy

Your information, handled with care.

Information we collect

When you contact Coreline Systems, we may collect your name, email address, selected project type, and the message you submit. If you use the subscription form, we collect your email address so we can manage that subscription request. The website also stores only your cookie-consent choice in browser storage.

How information is used

  • Responding to enquiries and messages.
  • Providing requested services and communicating with users.
  • Managing subscriptions where applicable.
  • Improving the website and services.

Contact forms

Information submitted through the contact form is sent to Coreline Systems for review and response. It is used for the enquiry you made and is not collected for unrelated purposes.

Subscriptions

The subscription form collects an email address and sends a notification to Coreline Systems when the email service is configured. Subscription delivery depends on that server-side email configuration; no separate marketing database is used by this site.

Third-party services

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and links to WhatsApp and LinkedIn. Contact and subscription notifications may be sent through the configured Gmail SMTP service. Those third parties have their own privacy policies and terms when you use their services.

Data security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect information submitted through this website. No online transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

User rights

You may contact Coreline Systems with questions about your personal information, request clarification about how it is used, or raise a privacy concern.

Policy updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary. The latest version will be made available on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@corelinesystem.co.zw or info@corelinesystem.co.zw.

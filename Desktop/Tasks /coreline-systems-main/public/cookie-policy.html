Cookie Policy

How this website uses cookies.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of information stored by a website in a visitor's browser or on their device. This website uses browser storage to remember your cookie-consent choice.

How Coreline Systems uses cookies

Coreline Systems does not currently use analytics, advertising, tracking, or other non-essential cookie services. The essential consent record is stored locally as coreline_cookie_consent with a value of accepted or denied.

Cookie consent

You can accept cookies or deny non-essential cookies using the banner. Your choice is saved so the banner does not appear on every visit.

Managing cookies

You can also manage or delete stored cookies and browser data through your browser settings. Removing this site's stored consent will show the consent banner again on your next visit.
